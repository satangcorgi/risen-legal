Verse identification

Risen uses on-device text recognition and, with your permission, optional cloud verse checking described in our Privacy Policy. The cloud service compares scanned text with the requested passage through Supabase and Cloudflare Workers AI. Identification can be unavailable, rate-limited or incorrect. On-device matching and the available typing fallback remain alternatives. Only scan content you have the right to use, and avoid including private information belonging to you or others.
