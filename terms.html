Risen is an alarm that stops when you open your Bible to a random verse, then offers a prayer or devotional on that verse and keeps the page in your Book. Risen is licensed to you under Apple's Standard End User License Agreement, together with these terms. By using Risen you agree to both.

Free and Premium

Risen is free to use, with one verse alarm, the full wake, a prayer with each verse, your notes, the whole Bible in three translations, and every verse you read kept in your Book (the last seven open in full). Risen Premium adds unlimited verse alarms, both prayers and devotionals, every page of your Book and printing it, every widget and the Live Activity, and all looks, icons and sounds.

Paying for Premium

Premium is offered weekly, yearly, or as Lifetime, a one-time purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Weekly and yearly are subscriptions: they renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. If a free trial is offered and you're eligible, payment is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings. Lifetime doesn't renew and is shared with your Family Sharing group. Refunds are handled by Apple.

If Premium ends, nothing is deleted. Your alarms keep ringing (the first one keeps its verse; others ring as plain alarms), and every verse you read stays in your Book.

Restore

Restore Premium on any device signed in to the same Apple Account, from the Premium page or Settings > Restore Purchases.

Using Risen

Risen is for personal, non-commercial use. Its Bible text comes from public-domain translations: the Berean Standard Bible, the World English Bible and the King James Version. The prayers and devotionals are original writing and may not be redistributed commercially without permission.

Finding the verse

Risen reads pages with on-device text recognition and, only if you allow it, online help described in the Privacy Policy. Recognition can be unavailable, limited or wrong; a photo or typing the verse always works. Only scan what you have the right to use, and keep private information out of the picture.

Alarms

Risen uses Apple's alarm system and is built to be reliable, but no app can promise your phone will wake you: a phone that's off, permissions that are denied or a system problem can stop an alarm. Don't rely on Risen as your only alarm when it matters.
