Risen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Risen is an alarm that stops when you open your Bible to a random verse, then opens a short prayer or devotional and keeps the page in your Book. Almost everything Risen knows about you stays on your phone. We don't sell your data, we don't track you across apps, and there's no Risen account.

On your phone

Your alarms, settings, the pages in your Book (date, verse, feeling, note), your Bible translation, your look and your onboarding answers are stored on your device. Risen doesn't upload them. Device backups may include them, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting Risen, or using Settings > Delete All Data, removes them from that phone. It doesn't delete backups, Apple or RevenueCat purchase records, or the service records described below.

Widgets and the Live Activity read a small summary (today's verse, how many verses you've read, your next alarm) that Risen writes to storage shared only with its own widget.

The camera and your page

Risen reads text from the camera, or from a photo you pick, using Apple's on-device text recognition. When a page matches your verse, Risen keeps a small dithered picture of that page on your phone for that day's page in your Book. It isn't uploaded. Turn it off in Settings > Keep a picture of your page, which also deletes the pictures already kept. A photo you pick stays in your Photos library. Share cards are saved to Photos only when you choose to save one.

Reading hard pages online (optional)

Risen first tries to match the page on your phone. The first time that fails outside an alarm, Risen asks whether it may get online help. If you allow it, and the phone can't match a page, Risen sends the words it read on that page (never the photo) and the text of the verse you're looking for, over an encrypted connection, to Risen's service on Supabase. The service sends those two texts to Anthropic's AI service (the Claude API), which compares their meaning and says whether they match. During an alarm Risen never stops to ask; it moves on to a photo or typing.

Anything readable in the frame can end up in the words Risen reads, so keep names and private notes out of the picture. To limit use and check access, the service also receives your IP address, a device identifier and your RevenueCat purchase identifier. It sends the purchase identifier to RevenueCat to check Premium. None of these identifiers go to the AI model.

Free devices get 3 online checks a day; Premium gets 30. The onboarding demo has its own small allowance. You can turn online help off or on in Settings > Read hard pages online. Turning it off stops future requests; it can't recall one already sent. On-device matching, photos and typing always work without it.

What the service keeps

The service doesn't store the text it reads, the verse, or the result in its database or logs. It keeps usage counts under keyed, pseudonymous identifiers, so it can count repeated use without storing the original identifiers.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Risen Premium (weekly, yearly, or Lifetime, which is a one-time purchase). RevenueCat manages access, receipt checks, fraud prevention and purchase reporting using a randomly assigned purchase identifier. It isn't your name or email. Risen never sees your card. Deleting Risen doesn't cancel a subscription; manage it in your iPhone's Settings. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and data processing terms.

Young users

Online help is optional and sends text off the phone. Anyone who needs a parent's consent should ask before turning it on; Risen works without it. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy questions or service data.

Your choices

Turn online help and page pictures on or off in Settings. Manage camera access in your iPhone's Settings. Write to us for questions or requests about service data; we may need your purchase identifier to find pseudonymous records. Some records may be kept to meet legal duties or prevent abuse, and we'll tell you what applies.
