Risen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Risen is a Christian alarm app: your alarm stops when you scan a Bible verse. This policy explains what data Risen handles. The short version: almost everything stays on your device, and we never sell or share your personal data.

No accounts

Risen has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your iPhone: your alarms and settings, your streak and reading history, your reflections and journal entries, the name you enter during onboarding, and your Bible version preference. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera

Risen uses your camera to recognize the Bible verse on the page in front of you. Frames are processed in memory on your device. No photos are saved, stored, or uploaded — ever.

Verse identification

When on-device recognition can't identify a verse, the extracted text (never an image) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which asks an AI model (Google Gemini) which Bible verse it is. The text is processed transiently to answer that one question and is not used to identify you. Requests include a random device identifier used only for rate limiting.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which handles subscription status using an anonymous identifier. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
